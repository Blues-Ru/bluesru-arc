в Нью-Мадриде, Массачусетс, родился Кэш МакКолл (Cash McCALL), певец и гитарист, сподвижник Уилли Диксона. Cash McCall (born Morris Dollison Jr., January 28, 1941 - April 20, 2019) was an American electric blues guitarist, singer and songwriter. He was best known for his 1966 R&B hit "When You Wake Up". Over his long career, his musical style evolved from gospel music to soul music to the blues.
