в Балтиморе, Мэрриленд, родился Фрэнк Заппа (Frank ZAPPA), гитарист, композитор, ироничный пересмешник. Frank Vincent Zappa (December 21, 1940 – December 4, 1993) was an American singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, composer, and bandleader. His work is characterized by nonconformity, free-form improvisation, sound experiments, musical virtuosity, and satire of American culture.
