в Далласе родился тенор-саксофонист <b>&quot;Твердоголовый&quot; Дэвид НЬЮМЭН</b>, участник многих блюзовых и джазовых сейшинов. David "Fathead" Newman (February 24, 1933 – January 20, 2009) was an American jazz and rhythm-and-blues saxophonist who made numerous recordings as a session musician and leader, but is best known for his work as a sideman on seminal 1950s and early 1960s recordings by singer-pianist Ray Charles.
