On this day, 1949, Theresa Needham, "The Godmother of the Chicago Blues", opened Theresa's Lounge in the basement of 4801 S. Indiana Ave in Chicago.  Junior Wells and Buddy Guy were regular performers there as well as part-time bar staff.  Muddy Waters, Little Walter, Otis Rush, Earl Hooker, Otis Spann, Jimmy Rogers, Howlin' Wolf and dozens of others all played there for just a $1 cover charge. Theresa's has been described as "wonderfully funky and friendly," "slightly dangerous," and as having "unreliable plumbing", but it was open 16 hours a day for 34 years.  Needham was posthumously inducted into the Blues Hall of Fame in 2001.
