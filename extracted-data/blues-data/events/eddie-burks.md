в Райзин-Сан, Миссиссиппи, родился Эдди Бёркс (Eddie BURKS), харпер, обосновавшийся по старой традиции в Чикаго. Eddie Burks (September 17, 1931 – January 27, 2005) was an American blues harmonica player and singer, well known for playing in Maxwell Street Market, Chicago, in the 1960s and 1970s, whose later career included a number of album releases, frequent touring, and work on the festival circuit.
