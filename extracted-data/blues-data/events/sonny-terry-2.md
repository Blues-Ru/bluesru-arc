Saunders Teddell or Saunders Terrell (or other variants, sources differ) (October 24, 1911 – March 11, 1986),[4] known as Sonny Terry, was an American Piedmont blues and folk musician,[1] who was known for his energetic blues harmonica style, which frequently included vocal whoops and hollers and occasionally imitations of trains and fox hunts.
