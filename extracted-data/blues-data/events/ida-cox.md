в Джорджии родилась  <a href="/BlackCat/ic.htm"><b>Айда Кокс</a> (Ida COX)</b>, прекраснейшая певица эпохи классического блюза. Ida Cox (born Ida M. Prather, February 26, 1888 or 1896 – November 10, 1967) was an American singer and vaudeville performer, best known for her blues performances and recordings. She was billed as "The Uncrowned Queen of the Blues".
