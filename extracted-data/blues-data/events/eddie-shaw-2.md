в Чикаго в возрасте 80 лет скончался Eddie Shaw (March 20, 1937 – January 29, 2018), an American Chicago blues tenor saxophonist, arranger and bandleader. He led Howlin' Wolf's band, the Wolf Gang, from 1972, both before Wolf's death in 1976 and subsequently.
