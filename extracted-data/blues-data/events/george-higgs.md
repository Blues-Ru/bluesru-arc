George Higgs (March 9, 1930 – January 29, 2013) was an American Piedmont blues acoustic guitarist, harmonicist, singer and songwriter.[2] He recorded three albums in his lifetime, although he spent over sixty years performing regularly, mainly in his home State.[3] In 1993, Higgs was granted the North Carolina Heritage Award from the North Carolina Arts Council.
