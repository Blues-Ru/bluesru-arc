Roy James Gaines (born August 12, 1934 or 1937 - 11 August, 2021) was an American Texas blues and electric blues guitarist, singer and songwriter. He wrote and recorded the song "A Hell of a Night", which was first issued on his 1982 album Gainelining. He is the younger brother of the blues musician Grady Gaines.
