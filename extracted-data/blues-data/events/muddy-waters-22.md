On this day, 1956, Muddy recorded, "Got My Mojo Working" for Chess Records in Chicago, Illinois.  It is one of 4 songs by Waters that the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame listed in its list of the 500 Songs that Shaped Rock and Roll.  It also won a Grammy Hall of Fame Award in 1999 and was inducted as a Classics of Blues Recording in the Blues Hall of Fame.
