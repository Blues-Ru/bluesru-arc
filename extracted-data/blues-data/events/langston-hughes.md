в Джоплине, Массачусетс, родился Лэнгстон Хьюз (Langston HUGHES). Поэт и публицист, борец за права афро-американцев, он в своих сочинениях прославлял и воспевал блюз. James Mercer Langston Hughes (February 1, 1901 – May 22, 1967) was an American poet, social activist, novelist, playwright, and columnist from Joplin, Missouri. One of the earliest innovators of the literary art form called jazz poetry, Hughes is best known as a leader of the Harlem Renaissance. He famously wrote about the period that "the Negro was in vogue", which was later paraphrased as "when Harlem was in vogue.
