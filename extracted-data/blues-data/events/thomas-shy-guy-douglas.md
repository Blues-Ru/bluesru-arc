Thomas "Shy Guy" Douglas (8 November 1917 - 19 October 1984 (aged 66)). "The late Nashville TN based blues singer and harmonica player Thomas "Shy Guy" Douglas had a rather long and continuous career in the local bluesmusic scene, making records for Excello, Bullet, Delta and MCA back in the day with a career spanning 1949-69. He was one of Nashville’s greatest Bluesmen (some figure he was the Greatest harp player in the Down Home blues genre)."
