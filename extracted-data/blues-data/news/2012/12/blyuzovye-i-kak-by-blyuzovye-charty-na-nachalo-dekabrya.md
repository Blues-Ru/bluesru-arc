<ol type="1"> <lh><u>Billboard Blues Charts</u></lh> <li>Gary Clark, Jr. - <b>Blak Blu</b> (Warner Brors)</li> <li>Bonnie Raitt - <b>Slipstream</b> (Redwing Records)</li> <li>Joe Bonamassa - <b>Beacon ater: Live From New York</b> (JR Adventures)</li> <li>Gary Clark, Jr. - <b> Bright Lights</b> EP (Warner Brors)</li> <li>Joe Bonamassa - <b>Driving Towards Daylight</b> (J&R Adventures)</li> <li>Tedeschi Trucks Band - <b>Everybody's Talkin'</b> (Sony Masterworks)</li> <li>Robert Cray - <b>Nothin But Love</b> (Provogue Records)</li> <li>Hugh Laurie - <b>Let m Talk</b> (Warner Brors)</li> <li>Cold Specks - <b>I Predict A Graceful Expulsion</b> (Mute Records)</li> <li>Tedeschi Trucks B - <b>Revelator</b> (Sony Masterworks)</li> </ol>
