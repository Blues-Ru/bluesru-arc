10 мая состоялась XXXIX церемония вручения премий Blues Music Award (бывшая W.C. Handy Award).

<ul> <lh>Complete list of Blues Music Award winners 2018:</lh> <li>Acoustic Album: Break the Chain - Doug MacLeod</li> <li>Acoustic Artist: Taj Mahal</li> <li>Album: TajMo - Taj Mahal & Keb' Mo'</li> <li>B. King Entertainer: Taj Mahal</li> <li>Band: Rick Estrin & the Nightcats</li> <li>Best Emerging Artist Album: Southern Avenue - Southern Avenue</li> <li>Contemporary Blues Album: TajMo - Taj Mahal & Keb' Mo'</li> <li>Contemporary Blues Female Artist: Samantha Fish</li> <li>Contemporary Blues Male Artist: Keb' Mo'</li> <li>Historical: A Legend Never Dies, Essential Recordings 1976-1997 - Luther Allison (Ruf Recordings)</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Vocalist: Beth Hart</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Bass: Michael "Mudcat" Ward</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Drums: Tony Braunagel</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Guitar: Ronnie Earl</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Harmonica: Jason Ricci</li> <li>Instrumentalist-Horn: Trombone Shorty</li> <li>Pinetop Perkins Piano Player (Instrumentalist - Piano): Victor Wainwright</li> <li>Koko Taylor Award (Traditional Blues Female): Ruthie Foster</li> <li>Rock Blues Album: We're All In This Together - Walter Trout</li> <li>Rock Blues Artist: Mike Zito</li> <li>Song: "The Blues Ain't Going Nowhere" written by Rick Estrin and performed by Rick Estrin</li> <li>Soul Blues Album: Robert Cray & Hi Rhythm - Robert Cray & Hi Rhythm</li> <li>Soul Blues Female Artist: Mavis Staples</li> <li>Soul Blues Male Artist: Curtis Salgado</li> <li>Traditional Blues Album: Right Place, Right Time - Mike Welch and Mike Ledbetter</li> <li>Traditional Blues Male Artist: Rick Estrin</li> </ul>
