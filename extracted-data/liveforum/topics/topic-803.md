## Mike's Place in Tel Aviv
**From:** Daniel Kriman (SOBO)  **Date:** 2003-05-01 01:04  **Post:** #12174

Privet vsem iz Israel,

Nasha komanda SOBO (www.sobo.co.il)igrala do poslednego momenta v Mike's Place kajdyu Friday, Assaf(Sammy) - lead vocals, bass - on je i hoz'ain Mike's Place,

iznachal'no Mike's Place bil zadyman kak underground movement,
myzika blues and rock still not that big here in Israel, 

kajdii kto kagda libo hot' odin raz posmotrel live show at Mike's ostavl'al v dyshe chast' togo ideala kotorim mi vse dishali.
Freedome, Rock-n-roll, LOVE

eto mesto poistini international - Amerikaneci, Anglichane, Izrail'tane, Rysskie, Rymini, Arabi, Kitaici, Avstraliici, voobshem vse kto hotite, - 
posmotrite na Mike's guestbook na www.mikesplacebars.com
posmotrite kak so vsego mira ludi pishyt...

Ya lichno znal pochti vseh postradavshih,
simvolichno to chto pogibli oficiantka, muzikant i slushatel'
(May they rest in peace)

Avi Tabib - nash geroy, eslib ne on, ne 3 a 30 pogibli bi...

May all our prays go to the good folks at Mike's Place - Israel
