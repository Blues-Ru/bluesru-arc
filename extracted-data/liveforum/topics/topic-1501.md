First the Birth of the Blues, Then the Fight Over Who Owns the Baby
September 28, 2003 By BRENT STAPLES 

The rap impresario Sean Combs, known as P. Diddy, had a
huge hit this fall as the lead producer of the soundtrack
for the movie "Bad Boys II, which included
the hot party tune "Shake Ya Tailfeather." Rap fans would
be surprised to know that the title dates back to the
Mississippi juke joints of the early 20th century, where
black field hands gathered to hear blues music after long
days in the cotton fields.
