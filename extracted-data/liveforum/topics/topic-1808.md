## Хм, старая песня.
**From:** Александр Гидревич (Alex Gidra)  **Date:** 2004-01-29 16:31  **Post:** #17008

Это старая песня и действительно folksong - "народная".
St. James Infirmary
Вариантов текста очень много, как у настоящей народной песни.
Вот вариант Кэба Кэллоуэя (см. Блюз Бразерс ч.1)

I'm went' down to St. James Infirmary, 
I saw  my baby there; 
She's stretched out on a long, white table, 
So cold, so pain, so fair. 

Let her go, let her go, God bless her, 
Wherever she may be, 
She can search this wide world over, 
never find another good lovin'man like me. 

Now, when I die, bury me in my straight-leg britches, 
Put on a box-back coat and a stetson hat, 
Twenty-dollar gold piece on my watch chain, 
So you can let all the boys know I died standing pat. 

Folks, now that you have heard my story, 
Say, boy, hand me another shot of that booze; 
If anyone should ask you, 
Tell 'em I've got those St. James Infirmary blues.
