## URGENT ANNOUNCEMENT: Moscow Red Square Concert Cancelled
**From:** S Kashin  **Date:** 2006-07-28 20:56  **Post:** #28059

Unfortunately the Eric Clapton performance scheduled to take place on August 3rd in Red Square, Moscow has been cancelled. Eric Clapton was delighted at the prospect of playing in Red Square before 20,000 of his Russian fans. The Russian promoter had been issued a permit for Eric Clapton to perform in Red Square signed by all appropriate Russian City and State authorities. However, earlier today, Russian officials withdrew the permit. Eric Clapton is extremely sorry to disappoint his numerous Russian fans but the circumstances of the cancellation are completely beyond his control. Ticket-holders should return their tickets to the box offices where they purchased the tickets for a refund.

http://www.whereseric.com/news/2006/07/moscow-red-square-concert-cancelled.html

торжество совпало с днем ВДВ, то организаторы решили совместить крестный ход
