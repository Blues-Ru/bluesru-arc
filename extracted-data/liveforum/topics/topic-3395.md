## Несколько рекомендаций
**From:** Muddy Waters  **Date:** 2006-09-11 18:42  **Post:** #28679

Если ознакомиться с самым настоящим и глубоким блюзом в золотое время 1945-1970, можно начинать примерно так 

Muddy Waters: The Best of Muddy Waters (Chess)
Drop Down Mama (сборная) (Chess)
Howlin' Wolf: "Moaning in the Moonlight" и "Howlin' Wolf" (Chess)
Sonny Boy Williamson: Down and Out Blues (иди best of) (Chess)
BB King: Live at the Regal или Singing the Blues (Ace/MCA)
T-Bone Walker: T-Bone's Blues (Atlantic)
Otis Rush: The Cobra Recordings
Magic Sam: West Side Soul (Delmark)
Junior Wells: Hoodoo Man Blues (Delmark)
Bobby Bland: The Best Of (on Duke records)
Lightnin' Hopkins: The Modern Recordings или the Herald Recordings 
Albert King: Born Under a Bad Sign или King of the Blues Guitar (Stax)
Mississippi Fred McDowell The Blues (Arhoolie)
John Lee Hooker: The Modern Recordings (Modern)
Elmore James: Best of the Early Years или любой альбом
Lowell Fulson: The Chess Recordings (Chess)
Little Walter: The Best of (Chess)
Jimmy Rogers: California Bound или The Best of
Freddy King: The best of (on Federal Records)
