## Animalisms - Tracklist
**From:** Alexei Perel (Mad_Pappa)  **Date:** 2002-02-03 04:36  **Post:** #600

Itak, poehali:-)) Zaranee proshu proscheniya za translit:-)))
Wo-perwih, kak i u mnogih komand togo wremeni, u Animals razlichayutsya nazwaniya UK i US releasow, da i pesni zachastuyu neskolko ranie whodyat w eti izdaniya. US-versiya etoi plastinki naziwaetsya Animalization, to zhe, chto w US bilo wipuscheno pod nazwaniem Animalisms - woobsche absolyutno drugaya plastinka, dowolno bolshoi raritet, w Ewrope i Australii ona wishla tolko w 90-h i naziwalas sootwetstwenno Blues Years i Roadrunners (hotya, poslednij wkluchaet w sebya i bolee pozdnie zapisi).
U menya w rukah sejchas Animalisms izdaniya 99 goda Repertoire, prekrasnoe kollekcionnoe pereizdanie s bonus-trackami. Itak:
Band: razumeetsya, Burdon, Chandler, Valentine, Rowberry, Steel i Jenkins (otmechenno otdelno). release - June 66'
Tracklist:
1.One Monkey Don't Stop No Show (Tex) Dowolno neobichnii mid-tempo opener dlya Animals. Ochen priyatno i ubeditelno.
2. Maudie (Hooker) - mid-tempo nomer, neskolko skuchnowataya wersiya, ma moj wzglyad.
3. Outcast (Cambell-Johnson)Dominiruet gitara Hiltona s izryadnoj dolej fuzz-a i tremolo. Ochen dinamichnij nomer. Oshushaetsya wliyanie Stax. Eric horosh, kak wsegda.
4. Sweet Little Sixteen - (Berry) ostaetsya oschuschenie nekotoroj nezawershennosti posle proslushiwaniya etoj pesni. Wpolne weroyatno, chto eto perwij i edinstwennij take. Eric w ocherednoj raz, kak i w Maudie , swobodno interpretiruet text, na etot raz upominaya Beatles w odnoj iz strochek.
5. You're on my mind(Burdon - Rowberry) - pop-ballada, Eric poet neobichno nizko, John igraetochen ubeditelno, organ i piano, gitari prakticheski ne slishno.
6. Clapping (Rowberry) - dowolno smelij dlya togo wremeni experiment - 1,5 minuti - kollazh iz hlopkow s priwkusom flamenco.
7. Gin House Blues (Troy-Henderso)- ne samaya luchshaya zapis Animals, s moei tochki zreniya. Nekotoraya neprodumannost aranzhirowki.  ZA udarnimi - Barry Jenkins. 
8. Squezze Her Tease Her (Tucker-Wilson) - priyatnij nomer up-tempo.
Eric kak wsegda na wisote. Slegka napominaet "Talkin' 'bout you".  Za udarnimi - Jenkins.
9. What Am I Liing For (Jay -Harris) - priyatnaya pop-balada. Horosh organ i Eric, kak obichno. 
10. I Put a Spell On You (Hawkins) - dowolno udachnaya wersiya etogo standarta. Wesma dinamichno. Gitarnoe solo wesma zabawno.  Eric richit prostrelennim zwerem. Smeni tempa tozhe wpechatlyayut. Organ sweden do minimuma.
11. That's All I Am To You (Blackwell-Scott) - up-tempo nomer s fuzz-gitaroj Hiltona i welikolepnoj tarelkoj Barry. Piano igraet nadezhnuyu podkladku. Eric - nuzhno li kommentirowat?:-)
12. She'll return It (Burdon - Rowberry) moshnij zawershayuschij nomer. Snowa Barry Jenkins za udarnimi.  W celom - ot wsej plastinki ostaetsya oschuschenie rosta Animals kak banda. Dalee - bonus -tracks

13. Inside Looking Out (J and A Lomax Burdon Chandler) klassicheskij single pozdnih Animals. Pererabotka pesni Rosie. Pod etim nazwaniem w repertuare komandi - s leta 65 goda.
14. Don't Bring Me Down (Goffin-King) ne menee izwestnij single Animals. Snowa fuzz-tremolo gitara, slilnij nalet soul. Klassika
15. Cheating (Burdon - Chandler) gipnotiziruyuschij nomer, postroennij na 2-h akkordah. Welikolepnaya dinamika. Snowa fuzz i tremolo na gitare. Partii instrumentow swedeni k minimumu. Eric - welikolepen.
16. Help Me Girl - (English-Weiss) - woobsche-to, perwij solnij single Burdona, ostalnie Animals w zapisi uchastiya ne prinimali, po prichine rospuska anda. Odnako, izdan kak "Eric Burdon and the New Animals".
17. See See Rider (trad, arr Rowberry) - Animals w nailuchshej forme. Hronologicheski - poslednij single banda. S moei tochki zreniya - luchshaya obrabotka etoj pesni. Riff, dinamika, breaks - wse na swoem meste.  Energiya wipleskiwatesya cherez kraj. Odna iz moih samih lyubimih pesen Animals.
18. I Just Wanna Make Love To You (Dixon) - s peroi EP, izdannoi "Graphic Sound Studio" za dengi samih Animals, esche do kontrakta s Decca. Release - October 63'.  Wposledstwii (w 66') pereizdano Decca na EP. Klassicheskij sostaw - s Steel-om i Price-om.  Ochen energichno, wersiya bolee blizka k Muddy, chem beshennogo tempa wersiya Stones.
20. Boom Boom (Hooker) odna iz , po-moemu, 3-h wersij etoi pesni. S togo zhe EP. Wmesto priwichnogo organa iz single-release - electro piano. Eric poet gorazdo bolee "po-chernomu", chem w drugih wersiyah. W nekotorih mestah parodiruet golos John-a Lee. Ochen horoshaya wersiya. Po-moemu, gorazdo bolee luchshaya, chem na posleduyuschem single.
21. Big Boss Man (Dixon -Smith) ta zhe EP. Price igraet na melodike, pitayas imitirowat harp. Zwuchit zabawno, no ne ochen ubeditelno.
22. Pretty Thing (McDaniels) s togo zhe EP. Snowa melodica. Na etot raz bolee ubeditelno, no na 2-h iz 4-h pesnyah? Nuzhno li dejstwitelno bilo brat ee wmesto signature-klawish? Eric snowa parodiruet vocal originala. Wsya wersiya ochen blizka k originalu.
23. Don't Bring Me Down
24.See See Rider
24 Help Me Girl
25. Cheating
Stereo - versii wisheopisannih trackow. S Amerikanskogo alboma Animalization. 
W celom - ochen horoshij disk, odin iz naibolee slushaemih mnoj na protyazhenii poslednego goda.
