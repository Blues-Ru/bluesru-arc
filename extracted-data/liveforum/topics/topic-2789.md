## Умер Крис Джонс...
**From:** Marat Dakota  **Date:** 2005-09-16 20:07  **Post:** #23211

With deep sorrow we regret to inform you, that Chris Jones died of lung cancer in the Albert-Schweitzer-Hospital in Northeim on Tuesday, September 13th 2005.

His funeral will take place in Germany in October, we will announce the time and place here as soon as possible.

"If this is the end of the line, you ain&#180;t gonna hear me cryin&#180; - it&#180;s been a damn good run."
      Chris Jones, 2005
