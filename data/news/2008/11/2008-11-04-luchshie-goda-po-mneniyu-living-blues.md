date: '2008-11-04'

---

Авторитетный журнал Living Blues уже 15 лет проводит собственное награждение "лучших" по итогам года. В последнем номере (#197) объявлены очередные лауреаты Living Blues Awards:

Blues Artist of the Year (Female)<br> Koko Taylor

Blues Artist of the Year (Male)<br> Lurrie Bell

Best Live Performer<br> Bobby Rush

Artist Deserving More Attention<br> Catherine Russell

Most Outstanding Blues Singer - Comeback Artist of the Year<br> Nappy Brown

Most Outstanding Musician (Guitar)<br> Lurrie Bell

Most Outstanding Musician (Keyboards)<br> Pinetop Perkins

Most Outstanding Musician (Harmonica)<br> James Cotton

Most Outstanding Musician (Bass)<br> Bob Stroger

Most Outstanding Musician (Drums)<br> Tie: Willie "Big Eyes" Smith & Kenny Smith

Most Outstanding Musician (Horns)<br> Big James Montgomery

Most Outstanding Musician (Other)<br> Aubrey Ghent - lap steel

Album of the Year<br> Nappy Brown - Long Time Coming (Blind Pig Records)

Best New Recording - Southern Soul<br> Denise LaSalle - Pay Before You Pump (Ecko Records)

Best New Recording - Best Debut<br> Albert White - Soul of the Blues (Music Maker)

Best New Recording - Contemporary Blues<br> Phillip Walker - Going Back Home (Delta Groove)

Best New Recording - Traditional & Acoustic<br> Bobby Rush - Raw (Deep Rush Records)

Best Historical Reissue - Prewar<br> Various Artists - Desperate Man Blues soundtrack (Dust To Digital)

Best Historical Reissue - Post-War<br> Robert Lockwood, Jr. - Steady Rolling Man (Delmark Records)

DVD of the Year<br> Various Artists - The American Folk-Blues Festival: The British Tours 1963-'66 (Hip-O)

Producer of the Year - New Recording<br> Randy Chortkoff - Delta Groove Productions

Producer of the Year - Historical/Reissue<br> Lance Ledbetter - Dust To Digital

Напомним, что Бобби Раш выступал в Москве в 1999 году, в программе Efes Blues Festival. А <a href="/artist/lurrie-bell/">Лури Белл</a> и <a href="/afisha/Stroger.htm">Боб Строгер</a> выступали в 2006 году в клубе <a href="http://www.roadhouse.ru/">"Дом у Дороги"</a>.
