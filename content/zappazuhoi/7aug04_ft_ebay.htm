Description
This was the original issue of “Lumpy Gravy” and the only issue for many years. Issued by Madman Earl Muntz for his revolutionary 4-track tape loop auto-stereo but immediately withdrawn for contractual reasons. Very few collectors even know of, or have seen, this issue, let alone have a copy. This one is near-mint, although time-worn. The 4-track format is similar to, but was promptly overshadowed by, the 8-track format. It can only be played on a 4-track cartridge player.
